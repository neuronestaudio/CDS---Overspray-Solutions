Local conditions

What your paint is up against in Bundoora.

The things that actually wreck a finish around here — and what we do about them.

Full sun, little shade

Newer Bundoora streets have young trees and wide open frontages, so cars sit in unbroken sun most of the day.

Industrial fallout

Airborne metal particle from nearby industry settles on horizontal panels and corrodes into the clear coat as tiny rust specks.

New cars, no protection

A lot of Bundoora cars are only a year or two old — the ideal time to seal paint, before the first wash marks go in.

Construction and estate dust

Building work around Bundoora puts render, cutting dust and paint mist into the air — and it bonds to clear coat rather than washing off.

FAQs

Bundoora ceramic coating questions.

Do you come to Bundoora, or do I bring the car to you?

All work is done at our Epping studio, about 9 km from Bundoora. A coating has to cure in a clean, controlled, dust-free space to reach its rated life, which is not something a driveway can offer. We are open 7 days by appointment.

There is building work around Bundoora. Can you remove construction overspray?

Yes — overspray and fallout removal is our specialty. Render, paint mist and concrete slurry bond to clear coat and washing does nothing. We remove it non-abrasively so the clear coat is preserved, then coat the car so it stops keying in.

How long does a ceramic coating take?

Most coatings are a one to three day job depending on how much paint correction is needed. We will give you an exact timeframe for your car when we assess it, and you are usually safe to drive the day after the coating goes on.

What does ceramic coating cost in Bundoora?

It is quoted per car, because the price is driven by the size of the car, the condition of the paint and how many correction stages it needs — not by your postcode. Tell us the car and what is bothering you and we will give you a straight number.
