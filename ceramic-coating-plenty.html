Local to Plenty

Plenty is semi-rural acreage on the edge of the growth corridor — big blocks, long driveways, plenty of dust.

Acreage driving means gravel, and gravel means abrasive dust over every panel plus stone chipping down low. Sealing the paint keeps the dust from bonding so it rinses rather than needing a wipe, which is what protects the finish long term. We coat wheels out here as standard — they take the worst of it.

Every Plenty booking is handled by Andy himself — owner-operated, 30+ years on paint, and the same standard whether it is a daily driver, a work ute or a weekend car. Tell us the car and what is bothering you and you will get a straight answer, not a package sell.

Local conditions

What your paint is up against in Plenty.

The things that actually wreck a finish around here — and what we do about them.

Freeway and Hume grime

Commuting from Plenty lays down an oily road film plus stone chipping across the front of the car.

Full sun, little shade

Newer Plenty streets have young trees and wide open frontages, so cars sit in unbroken sun most of the day.

Industrial fallout

Airborne metal particle from nearby industry settles on horizontal panels and corrodes into the clear coat as tiny rust specks.

New cars, no protection

A lot of Plenty cars are only a year or two old — the ideal time to seal paint, before the first wash marks go in.

The process

How a Plenty booking runs.

01

Assessment

We look at your paint under proper lighting and measure the clear coat, then tell you what it actually needs.

02

Decontaminate

Iron-fallout treatment and a clay strip, so nothing is ever sealed in under the coating.

03

Correct

Machine polishing in measured stages to remove swirls and etching rather than fill them.

04

Coat & cure

GYEON or ONYX applied and cured in a controlled, dust-free space, with the warranty registered.

FAQs

Plenty ceramic coating questions.

Do you come to Plenty, or do I bring the car to you?

All work is done at our Epping studio, about 9 km from Plenty. A coating has to cure in a clean, controlled, dust-free space to reach its rated life, which is not something a driveway can offer. We are open 7 days by appointment.

There is building work around Plenty. Can you remove construction overspray?

Yes — overspray and fallout removal is our specialty. Render, paint mist and concrete slurry bond to clear coat and washing does nothing. We remove it non-abrasively so the clear coat is preserved, then coat the car so it stops keying in.

How long does a ceramic coating take?

Most coatings are a one to three day job depending on how much paint correction is needed. We will give you an exact timeframe for your car when we assess it, and you are usually safe to drive the day after the coating goes on.

What does ceramic coating cost in Plenty?

It is quoted per car, because the price is driven by the size of the car, the condition of the paint and how many correction stages it needs — not by your postcode. Tell us the car and what is bothering you and we will give you a straight number.
