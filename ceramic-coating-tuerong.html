Looking after cars in Tuerong

Tuerong is big blocks and long gravel driveways, and that means dust. A fine abrasive film settles on the car every time it moves, and it is the reason acreage cars get scratched by the very act of cleaning them.

There is no sales team and no subcontracting. Andy assesses, corrects and coats every car from Tuerong himself, with more than thirty years on paint behind him, and will tell you honestly if a job is not worth doing.

Lifestyle acreage

What your paint is up against in Tuerong.

Sun on open blocks

Open acreage means little shade and a lot of UV, which fades paint and chalks plastic trim.

Sheds and farm dust

Cars stored in sheds pick up hay dust, bird droppings from roosting birds and the odd rodent - interiors included.

Long kilometres

Acreage means driving further for everything. Bugs, road film and stone chips add up faster than they do in the suburbs.

Gravel-drive dust

Every trip up an unsealed drive leaves a layer of fine, abrasive dust. Wiping it off dry is like using very fine sandpaper.

FAQs

Before you book from Tuerong.

Mobile or studio - which suits a Tuerong car?

CDS is mobile across Melbourne's south-east, Tuerong included, and also runs a studio in Berwick (36 km from you) if you would rather drop the car in. Acreage properties usually have the room, the shed and the power a mobile job needs, so on-site work suits most of what we do out here. Mention your parking set-up when you enquire and Andy will say honestly which way gives the better result.

Do you coat utes and 4WDs?

Yes, including trays, canopies, bars and raw plastics. They benefit as much as the paint does.

Will a coating stop stone chips?

No. Stone chips are impact damage and only paint protection film meaningfully reduces them. A coating seals the paint around them.

Can you clean out a car that lives in a shed?

Yes. Interior detailing handles dust, droppings and odour, and coating the fabric makes it easier to keep clean afterwards.
