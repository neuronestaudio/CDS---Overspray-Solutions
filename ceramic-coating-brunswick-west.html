Local to Brunswick West

Brunswick West mixes older homes with newer infill, and most of those new builds came without much off-street parking. The result is a lot of good cars living on the street.

Outdoor parking is really a UV problem. Melbourne sun flattens red and black paint over a few summers, and once the clear coat oxidises there is no wax that brings it back. A coating is a UV-stable barrier that slows that down; where the damage is already done, our restoration polish cuts back the dead layer and brings the colour up before we seal it.

Every Brunswick West booking is handled by Andy himself — owner-operated, 30+ years on paint, and the same standard whether it is a daily driver, a work ute or a weekend car. Tell us the car and what is bothering you and you will get a straight answer, not a package sell.

FAQs

Brunswick West ceramic coating questions.

Do you come to Brunswick West, or do I bring the car to you?

All work is done at our Epping studio, about 15 km from Brunswick West. A coating has to cure in a clean, controlled, dust-free space to reach its rated life, which is not something a driveway can offer. We are open 7 days by appointment.

My car is parked on the street in Brunswick West — is a coating still worth it?

That is exactly when it is most worth it. A kerbside car takes UV, sap and bird droppings with no shelter, and a coating seals the clear coat so that mess sits on the surface and lifts in a normal wash instead of etching in.

How long does a ceramic coating take?

Most coatings are a one to three day job depending on how much paint correction is needed. We will give you an exact timeframe for your car when we assess it, and you are usually safe to drive the day after the coating goes on.

What does ceramic coating cost in Brunswick West?

It is quoted per car, because the price is driven by the size of the car, the condition of the paint and how many correction stages it needs — not by your postcode. Tell us the car and what is bothering you and we will give you a straight number.
