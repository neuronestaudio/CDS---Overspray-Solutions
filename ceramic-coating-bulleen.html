Local to Bulleen

Bulleen sits on the river flats with market gardens nearby, which puts both dust and heavy tree cover over the same streets.

You get airborne soil dust from the flats and sap from the established trees, and the two combine badly — grit sitting on a sticky surface is abrasive the moment anyone wipes it. Sealing the paint means it rinses instead, which is the single biggest thing you can do for a car parked here.

FAQs

Bulleen ceramic coating questions.

Do you come to Bulleen, or do I bring the car to you?

All work is done at our Epping studio, about 16 km from Bulleen. A coating has to cure in a clean, controlled, dust-free space to reach its rated life, which is not something a driveway can offer. We are open 7 days by appointment.

Gum trees hang over my driveway in Bulleen. Will a coating stop sap damage?

It will not stop sap landing, but it stops it bonding. On sealed paint the sap sits on the surface, so you can remove it safely instead of finding an etched ring weeks later. Any rings already in the clear coat are polished out first.

How long does a ceramic coating take?

Most coatings are a one to three day job depending on how much paint correction is needed. We will give you an exact timeframe for your car when we assess it, and you are usually safe to drive the day after the coating goes on.

What does ceramic coating cost in Bulleen?

It is quoted per car, because the price is driven by the size of the car, the condition of the paint and how many correction stages it needs — not by your postcode. Tell us the car and what is bothering you and we will give you a straight number.
