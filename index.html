Why ceramic coating

More than a shine — real protection.

A ceramic coating is a liquid nano-coating that bonds to your clear coat: a hard, ultra-slick sacrificial shell that shields the paint underneath and makes the whole car easier to live with. Here's what it actually does for yours.

Years of protection

Bonds tightly to the paint and outlasts traditional waxes and sealants by years, not months.

Hydrophobic

Rain and water bead up and sheet straight off, carrying dirt and grime away with them.

Easier to wash

Dirt, mud and road salt struggle to stick, so every wash is faster, safer and needed less often.

UV & fade defence

Blocks the UV radiation that oxidises paint and leaves it looking dull, chalky and faded.

Stain resistance

Bird droppings, bug splatter, tree sap and acidic fallout are far less likely to etch the clear coat.

Deeper gloss

Magnifies the reflectivity of your paint for a rich, wet, mirror-like finish that turns heads.

No more waxing

No re-waxing or resealing every few months — one professional coat does the job for years.

Holds resale value

Keeps the paint sharp and pristine, so the car presents better and holds its value longer.

Straight up

A ceramic coating is a sacrificial shield, not armour. It won't stop heavy rock chips or deep car-park scratches — that's what paint protection film (PPF) is for. What it will do is keep your paint slicker, glossier and far easier to clean, and protect it from the everyday things that wreck a finish.

The coating process

Four stages,
one flawless finish.

A proper ceramic coating is a process, not a bottle in a box. Here is exactly how your car goes from tired paint to a sealed, mirror finish.

Stage 01

Decontaminate

Every coating starts on a spotless surface. A thorough wash, iron-fallout and tar removal, then a clay treatment strip out bonded contamination — so nothing is ever sealed in under the coating.

Stage 02

Correct

Multi-stage machine polishing removes swirls, scratches and oxidation, pulling the true depth and gloss back out of the paint — because a coating locks in whatever is underneath it.

Stage 03

Coat

The GYEON or ONYX ceramic is applied by hand, panel by panel, and levelled — bonding a hard, hydrophobic, chemical-resistant layer to your clear coat for years of protection.

Stage 04

Protect

The coating cures in a clean, controlled, dust-free studio. You drive away with a written manufacturer warranty and a simple maintenance plan to keep it beading and glossy for the long haul.
