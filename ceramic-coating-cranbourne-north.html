New estates and growth corridors

What Cranbourne North does to a car’s finish.

Young trees, full sun

New streets have little shade, so cars sit in unbroken sun most of the day. UV is what turns dark paint flat over a few summers.

Mud on unfinished roads

Temporary roads and unsealed lots mean mud and clay thrown up the sills and wheel arches, which bakes on if it is left.

Dealer protection packages

Many new cars from the area arrive with a dealer 'protection' spray that lasts months, not years. A professionally applied coating is a different product.

Industrial fallout from transport

New cars often arrive with rail and transport fallout already bonded to the paint from shipping. It is removed before anything is sealed over it.

FAQs

Cranbourne North ceramic coating questions.

Can the work be done at my place in Cranbourne North?

Andy is mobile as well as studio-based, so the work can be done at your place in Cranbourne North or at the Berwick studio, about 6 km away. In a new estate the issue is dust from the building sites around you. A mobile job is done with the garage door down for that reason; if the garage is still full of boxes, the studio is the cleaner option. Tell us where the car is kept when you book and you will get a straight answer on which suits the job.

How soon should I deal with overspray?

As soon as you notice it. Fresh overspray lifts far more easily than overspray that has baked on through a hot week.

Can you do the glass and wheels too?

Yes. Glass picks up just as much site dust as paint, and coated wheels stop brake dust and mud baking on.

There is building work in my street. Can you remove overspray?

Yes - it is our specialty. Overspray, render and concrete spatter are removed non-abrasively so the clear coat is preserved, then the paint is coated so the next round of site dust washes off.
