Chelsea Heights, and what it does to paint

Chelsea Heights is established family country - the kind of suburb where a car does school runs, weekend sport and the supermarket, often for a decade. That is hard, honest wear, and most of it is surface damage that correction can take back out.

Every Chelsea Heights booking is handled by Andy himself — owner-operated, 30+ years on paint, and the same standard for a daily driver, a work ute or a weekend car. Tell us the car and what is bothering you and you will get a straight answer.

Established family suburbs

The things that wear paint down in Chelsea Heights.

Fading on the horizontal panels

Roofs, bonnets and boot lids take the most sun. On reds and dark colours that shows up as a flat, chalky finish that a wash no longer shifts.

Interior wear from family life

Food, drinks, muddy boots and dog hair. Interior coatings seal fabric and leather so spills can be blotted up instead of soaking in.

Door dings and car park marks

Shopping-centre car parks leave light scuffs and transfer on door edges. Transfer can usually be polished off; a coating makes the next one easier to remove.

Brake dust on busy wheels

Stop-start suburban driving loads wheels with hot brake dust that bakes into the finish. Coated wheels rinse clean instead.

The process

How a Chelsea Heights booking runs.

01

Assessment

We inspect the paint under proper lighting and measure the clear coat, then tell you what it actually needs.

02

Decontaminate

Iron-fallout treatment and a clay strip, so nothing is ever sealed in under the coating.

03

Correct

Machine polishing in measured stages to remove swirls and etching rather than fill them.

04

Coat & cure

GYEON or ONYX applied panel by panel and cured under cover — in the studio, or in your garage on a mobile job — with the warranty registered.

FAQs

Questions we get from Chelsea Heights.

Is CDS mobile in Chelsea Heights?

Andy works mobile in Chelsea Heights and from the Berwick studio, which is about 19 km from you, so it is your choice. Most family homes have a garage or carport, which is all a mobile job needs, and it saves you juggling a second car for the drop-off. Either way it is the same person, the same products and the same registered warranty.

How long will I be without the car?

Most coatings are a one to three day job depending on how much correction the paint needs. You will get an exact timeframe when we assess it.

My roof has gone chalky. Can that be fixed?

If the clear coat is oxidised but still intact, a restoration polish usually brings the colour back. If it has started to peel, no polish will fix it and we will tell you straight.

Do I need to change how I wash the car?

Just avoid automatic brush washes and use a proper car shampoo. The coating makes everything else easier.
