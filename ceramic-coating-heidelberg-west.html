Local to Heidelberg West

Heidelberg West sits alongside a working industrial pocket, and that changes what lands on your paint compared with the suburbs either side of it.

Industrial fallout is the real problem here — fine airborne metal that lands on horizontal panels and starts rusting into the clear coat. You feel it as roughness on a clean bonnet. It needs a chemical iron-fallout treatment and a clay strip to remove properly, which is exactly the work our overspray side does every day. Once the paint is clean, a coating stops it bonding again.

FAQs

Heidelberg West ceramic coating questions.

Do you come to Heidelberg West, or do I bring the car to you?

All work is done at our Epping studio, about 12 km from Heidelberg West. A coating has to cure in a clean, controlled, dust-free space to reach its rated life, which is not something a driveway can offer. We are open 7 days by appointment.

Gum trees hang over my driveway in Heidelberg West. Will a coating stop sap damage?

It will not stop sap landing, but it stops it bonding. On sealed paint the sap sits on the surface, so you can remove it safely instead of finding an etched ring weeks later. Any rings already in the clear coat are polished out first.

How long does a ceramic coating take?

Most coatings are a one to three day job depending on how much paint correction is needed. We will give you an exact timeframe for your car when we assess it, and you are usually safe to drive the day after the coating goes on.

What does ceramic coating cost in Heidelberg West?

It is quoted per car, because the price is driven by the size of the car, the condition of the paint and how many correction stages it needs — not by your postcode. Tell us the car and what is bothering you and we will give you a straight number.
