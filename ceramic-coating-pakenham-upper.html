Pakenham Upper, and what it does to paint

Acreage living around Pakenham Upper is hard on a car's finish: unsealed drives, dirt roads, sheds, horse floats and the ute that does the real work. Most of the damage we see is from grit - and grit is exactly what a coating is best at shrugging off.

Lifestyle acreage

The things that wear paint down in Pakenham Upper.

Long kilometres

Acreage means driving further for everything. Bugs, road film and stone chips add up faster than they do in the suburbs.

Gravel-drive dust

Every trip up an unsealed drive leaves a layer of fine, abrasive dust. Wiping it off dry is like using very fine sandpaper.

Stone chips from dirt roads

Loose gravel flicks up into the front and the sills. Coatings do not stop impacts, but they seal the surrounding paint and make chips less of an entry point.

Utes that work

Farm and trade utes carry dirt, feed, chemicals and tools. A coated tray, canopy and paint takes a fraction of the time to clean.

FAQs

Is CDS mobile in Pakenham Upper?

Andy works mobile in Pakenham Upper and from the Berwick studio, which is about 14 km from you, so it is your choice. Acreage properties usually have the room, the shed and the power a mobile job needs, so on-site work suits most of what we do out here. Can you clean out a car that lives in a shed?

Yes. Interior detailing handles dust, droppings and odour, and coating the fabric makes it easier to keep clean afterwards.

How should I wash a car that gets dusty every day?

Rinse first with plenty of water to lift the grit, then wash - never wipe dust off dry. On coated paint the rinse does most of the job.

Can you coat the wheels?

Yes, and on dirt roads it is well worth it - brake dust and mud stop baking onto coated wheels.
