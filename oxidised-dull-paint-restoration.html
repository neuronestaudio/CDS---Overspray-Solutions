Oxidised & Dull Paint Restoration

Chalky, faded, oxidised paint is dead clear coat sitting on top of good colour — cut that back with machine polishing and the colour and gloss come straight back.

Get a free quote Call 0410 939 700

Melbourne sun is hard on neglected paint. Oxidation is the clear coat breaking down on the surface — it goes chalky, flat and pale. As long as there's enough clear coat left, machine correction removes that dead layer and reveals the healthy paint underneath.

  • Cuts back sun-baked oxidation and chalkiness
  • Restores colour depth and reflection
  • Best sealed with a coating to stop it returning
  • Great value before selling a tired car

The transformation on a badly oxidised bonnet or roof is dramatic. To keep it, we seal the restored paint with a coating so the UV can't chew it up again — particularly worth it on reds and dark colours.
