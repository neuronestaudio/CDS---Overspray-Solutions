What your paint is up against in Wantirna South.

Door dings and car park marks

Shopping-centre car parks leave light scuffs and transfer on door edges. Transfer can usually be polished off; a coating makes the next one easier to remove.

Brake dust on busy wheels

Stop-start suburban driving loads wheels with hot brake dust that bakes into the finish. Coated wheels rinse clean instead.

Keeping a long-term car presentable

If the plan is to keep the car for years, protecting it once properly costs less than repeated polishing that thins the clear coat each time.

Quick-wash swirls

Fast washes with one bucket and whatever towel is closest put circular marring into the paint that only shows in the sun. It is the single most common thing we correct.

Can you get rid of the swirl marks?

Yes - swirls are fine scratches in the clear coat, and machine correction levels them out. The coating afterwards is what stops them coming straight back.

Can you do the inside as well?

Yes. We deep-clean and coat leather, fabric and trim so spills, dye transfer and grime stop soaking in.
