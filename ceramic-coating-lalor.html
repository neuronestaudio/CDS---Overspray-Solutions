Local to Lalor

Lalor is older, established and tightly built, with a lot of cars living on the street under mature trees rather than in a garage.

Kerbside parking under trees means sap and bird droppings landing on the paint continuously, and both etch once they bake on in summer. A coating keeps the surface sealed so the residue sits on top and lifts in a wash. Where it has already etched a ring into the clear coat, we polish that out first.

The process

How a Lalor booking runs.

01

Assessment

We look at your paint under proper lighting and measure the clear coat, then tell you what it actually needs.

FAQs

Lalor ceramic coating questions.

Do you come to Lalor, or do I bring the car to you?

All work is done at our Epping studio, about 3 km from Lalor. A coating has to cure in a clean, controlled, dust-free space to reach its rated life, which is not something a driveway can offer. We are open 7 days by appointment.

My car is parked on the street in Lalor — is a coating still worth it?

That is exactly when it is most worth it. A kerbside car takes UV, sap and bird droppings with no shelter, and a coating seals the clear coat so that mess sits on the surface and lifts in a normal wash instead of etching in.

How long does a ceramic coating take?

Most coatings are a one to three day job depending on how much paint correction is needed. We will give you an exact timeframe for your car when we assess it, and you are usually safe to drive the day after the coating goes on.

What does ceramic coating cost in Lalor?

It is quoted per car, because the price is driven by the size of the car, the condition of the paint and how many correction stages it needs — not by your postcode. Tell us the car and what is bothering you and we will give you a straight number.
