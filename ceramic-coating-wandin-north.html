FAQs

Wandin North ceramic coating questions.

Do you come to Wandin North, or do I bring the car in?

We can come to you in Wandin North, or you can bring the car to the studio in Berwick, roughly 30 km away - both are on offer. A shed or a closed garage is ideal for a mobile job. Some owners would rather tie it in with a trip into town and leave the car at the studio instead. If you are unsure, send a photo of where the car lives with your enquiry and we will work out the best option.

Will a coating help with the mud and dust?

Yes. Neither bonds to sealed paint the way it does to bare clear coat, so a hose does most of the cleaning.

Can you clean up a working interior?

Yes. We deep-clean seats, carpets and trim, and coat the fabric so dirt and spills are easier to deal with next time.

What about chalky, faded trim?

Faded plastic trim can usually be restored and then coated so it does not go grey again.
