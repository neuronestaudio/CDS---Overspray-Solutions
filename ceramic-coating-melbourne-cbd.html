What your paint is up against in Melbourne CBD.

The things that actually wreck a finish around here — and what we do about them.

Street parking, all year

Most Melbourne CBD cars live on the kerb rather than in a garage, so paint takes UV all summer and damp all winter with nowhere to hide.

Tree sap and droppings

Established street trees over Melbourne CBD mean sap and bird mess land constantly — both etch into clear coat once they bake on.

Fast washes, fine swirls

Cars washed quickly between work and weekends pick up the circular marring you only notice when the sun hits it.

Traffic film

Close-in suburbs collect an oily brake-dust and exhaust film that bonds to bare paint and makes a car look dirty days after a wash.

The process

How a Melbourne CBD booking runs.

01

Assessment

We look at your paint under proper lighting and measure the clear coat, then tell you what it actually needs.

02

Decontaminate

Iron-fallout treatment and a clay strip, so nothing is ever sealed in under the coating.

03

Machine polishing in measured stages to remove swirls and etching rather than fill them.

FAQs

Melbourne CBD ceramic coating questions.

Do you come to Melbourne CBD, or do I bring the car to you?

All work is done at our Epping studio, about 21 km from Melbourne CBD. A coating has to cure in a clean, controlled, dust-free space to reach its rated life, which is not something a driveway can offer. We are open 7 days by appointment.

My car is parked on the street in Melbourne CBD — is a coating still worth it?

That is exactly when it is most worth it. A kerbside car takes UV, sap and bird droppings with no shelter, and a coating seals the clear coat so that mess sits on the surface and lifts in a normal wash instead of etching in.

How long does a ceramic coating take?

Most coatings are a one to three day job depending on how much paint correction is needed. We will give you an exact timeframe for your car when we assess it, and you are usually safe to drive the day after the coating goes on.

What does ceramic coating cost in Melbourne CBD?

It is quoted per car, because the price is driven by the size of the car, the condition of the paint and how many correction stages it needs — not by your postcode. Tell us the car and what is bothering you and we will give you a straight number.
