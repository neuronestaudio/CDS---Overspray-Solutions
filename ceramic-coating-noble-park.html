Busy, dense and kerbside

Local conditions in Noble Park, and how we handle them.

Bird droppings in town

Pigeons and street trees mean frequent direct hits. Acidic droppings etch fast in summer; coated paint lets them wipe off.

Interior grime from daily use

Commuting, takeaway and passengers wear interiors quickly. Interior coatings keep fabric and leather from soaking up spills.

Traffic film

Exhaust and brake residue from heavy traffic forms an oily film that bonds to unprotected paint and makes a car look grubby within days of a wash.

Kerbside scuffs

Street parking means brushed flanks, transfer on bumper corners and door-edge chips. Transfer usually polishes off; a coating makes the next one easier.

FAQs

Questions we get from Noble Park.

Do you come to Noble Park, or do I bring the car in?

We can come to you in Noble Park, or you can bring the car to the studio in Berwick, roughly 17 km away - both are on offer. It comes down to parking. With a garage or an undercover space and access to power, mobile works well. If the car lives on the street, the studio is the better place for a coating to cure. If you are unsure, send a photo of where the car lives with your enquiry and we will work out the best option.

Will a coating stop door dings?

No - nothing liquid stops an impact. It resists wash marring, etching and grime; for impact protection on high-risk areas, paint protection film is the right product.

Why does my car look dirty so soon after a wash?

That is traffic film re-bonding to bare paint. On coated paint it cannot key in the same way, so the car stays cleaner for longer.

Do you do wheels and glass as well?

Yes. Coated wheels stop brake dust baking on, and coated glass sheds rain at speed and cuts night glare.
