What your paint is up against in Donvale.

The things that actually wreck a finish around here — and what we do about them.

Eucalypt sap

Gums over Donvale driveways drop a sticky, acidic sap that sets hard and etches a ring into clear coat within a hot day.

Heavy native tree cover means debris sitting on the paint constantly, holding moisture against it.

Gravel driveways and unmade roads around Donvale put abrasive dust over the car — and wiping it dry is what scratches bonnets.

Shaded Donvale streets stay damp longer, which gives sap and fallout more time to bite into the surface.

FAQs

Donvale ceramic coating questions.

Do you come to Donvale, or do I bring the car to you?

All work is done at our Epping studio, about 23 km from Donvale. A coating has to cure in a clean, controlled, dust-free space to reach its rated life, which is not something a driveway can offer. We are open 7 days by appointment.

Gum trees hang over my driveway in Donvale. Will a coating stop sap damage?

It will not stop sap landing, but it stops it bonding. On sealed paint the sap sits on the surface, so you can remove it safely instead of finding an etched ring weeks later. Any rings already in the clear coat are polished out first.

How long does a ceramic coating take?

Most coatings are a one to three day job depending on how much paint correction is needed. We will give you an exact timeframe for your car when we assess it, and you are usually safe to drive the day after the coating goes on.

What does ceramic coating cost in Donvale?

It is quoted per car, because the price is driven by the size of the car, the condition of the paint and how many correction stages it needs — not by your postcode. Tell us the car and what is bothering you and we will give you a straight number.
