Local to Doreen

Doreen sits on the rural fringe of the growth corridor — new estates on one side, paddocks and unsealed edges on the other.

That mix means fine airborne dust settling on the car constantly, and dust is abrasive. Wiping it off a dry panel is how bonnets get scratched. On a coated car the dust does not bond, so it rinses away under water pressure instead of needing to be wiped — which is what actually protects the finish out here.

FAQs

Doreen ceramic coating questions.

Do you come to Doreen, or do I bring the car to you?

All work is done at our Epping studio, about 13 km from Doreen. A coating has to cure in a clean, controlled, dust-free space to reach its rated life, which is not something a driveway can offer. We are open 7 days by appointment.

There is building work around Doreen. Can you remove construction overspray?

Yes — overspray and fallout removal is our specialty. Render, paint mist and concrete slurry bond to clear coat and washing does nothing. We remove it non-abrasively so the clear coat is preserved, then coat the car so it stops keying in.

How long does a ceramic coating take?

Most coatings are a one to three day job depending on how much paint correction is needed. We will give you an exact timeframe for your car when we assess it, and you are usually safe to drive the day after the coating goes on.

What does ceramic coating cost in Doreen?

It is quoted per car, because the price is driven by the size of the car, the condition of the paint and how many correction stages it needs — not by your postcode. Tell us the car and what is bothering you and we will give you a straight number.
