Local to Mill Park

Mill Park is established family territory — the estates built through the 80s and 90s, wide streets and a lot of cars that have been in one household a long time.

Long-owned cars almost always have sound paint hiding under a decade of light wash marring. That is the best case for correction: the depth is still in the clear coat, it is just being scattered by fine scratches. We cut that back and seal it, and the colour reads the way it did when it was new.

Every Mill Park booking is handled by Andy himself — owner-operated, 30+ years on paint, and the same standard whether it is a daily driver, a work ute or a weekend car. Tell us the car and what is bothering you and you will get a straight answer, not a package sell.

The process

How a Mill Park booking runs.

01

Assessment

We look at your paint under proper lighting and measure the clear coat, then tell you what it actually needs.

02

Decontaminate

Iron-fallout treatment and a clay strip, so nothing is ever sealed in under the coating.

03

Correct

Machine polishing in measured stages to remove swirls and etching rather than fill them.

Coat & cure

FAQs

Mill Park ceramic coating questions.

Do you come to Mill Park, or do I bring the car to you?

All work is done at our Epping studio, about 6 km from Mill Park. A coating has to cure in a clean, controlled, dust-free space to reach its rated life, which is not something a driveway can offer. We are open 7 days by appointment.

There is building work around Mill Park. Can you remove construction overspray?

Yes — overspray and fallout removal is our specialty. Render, paint mist and concrete slurry bond to clear coat and washing does nothing. We remove it non-abrasively so the clear coat is preserved, then coat the car so it stops keying in.

How long does a ceramic coating take?

Most coatings are a one to three day job depending on how much paint correction is needed. We will give you an exact timeframe for your car when we assess it, and you are usually safe to drive the day after the coating goes on.

What does ceramic coating cost in Mill Park?

It is quoted per car, because the price is driven by the size of the car, the condition of the paint and how many correction stages it needs — not by your postcode. Tell us the car and what is bothering you and we will give you a straight number.
