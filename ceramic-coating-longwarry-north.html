The process

How a Longwarry North booking runs.

01

Assessment

We inspect the paint under proper lighting and measure the clear coat, then tell you what it actually needs.

02

Decontaminate

Iron-fallout treatment and a clay strip, so nothing is ever sealed in under the coating.

03

Correct

Machine polishing in measured stages to remove swirls and etching rather than fill them.

04

Coat & cure

GYEON or ONYX applied panel by panel and cured under cover — in the studio, or in your garage on a mobile job — with the warranty registered.

FAQs

Longwarry North ceramic coating questions.

Can the work be done at my place in Longwarry North?

Andy is mobile as well as studio-based, so the work can be done at your place in Longwarry North or at the Berwick studio, about 38 km away. A shed or a closed garage is ideal for a mobile job. Some owners would rather tie it in with a trip into town and leave the car at the studio instead. Tell us where the car is kept when you book and you will get a straight answer on which suits the job.

Do you coat farm utes?

Yes - paint, trays, canopies and trim. A coated ute rinses clean after a day on the tracks.

Can you get bug marks off the front?

Fresh bug residue comes off easily; etched marks can usually be polished out. On coated paint they barely stick.

Will a coating help with the mud and dust?

Yes. Neither bonds to sealed paint the way it does to bare clear coat, so a hose does most of the cleaning.
