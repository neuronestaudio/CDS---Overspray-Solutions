Local to Coburg North

Coburg North sits right on the Hume corridor, which means a steady diet of freeway grime, truck spray and brake dust on anything parked nearby.

Freeway film is not just dirt — it is oily, and it bonds. Owners here tell us the car looks dirty again two days after a wash, and that is the film re-sticking to bare clear coat. A coating changes the surface tension so the road grime does not key in, and a rinse gets most of it off. We decontaminate first so we are not sealing that film in.

FAQs

Coburg North ceramic coating questions.

Do you come to Coburg North, or do I bring the car to you?

All work is done at our Epping studio, about 10 km from Coburg North. A coating has to cure in a clean, controlled, dust-free space to reach its rated life, which is not something a driveway can offer. We are open 7 days by appointment.

My car is parked on the street in Coburg North — is a coating still worth it?

That is exactly when it is most worth it. A kerbside car takes UV, sap and bird droppings with no shelter, and a coating seals the clear coat so that mess sits on the surface and lifts in a normal wash instead of etching in.

How long does a ceramic coating take?

Most coatings are a one to three day job depending on how much paint correction is needed. We will give you an exact timeframe for your car when we assess it, and you are usually safe to drive the day after the coating goes on.

What does ceramic coating cost in Coburg North?

It is quoted per car, because the price is driven by the size of the car, the condition of the paint and how many correction stages it needs — not by your postcode. Tell us the car and what is bothering you and we will give you a straight number.
