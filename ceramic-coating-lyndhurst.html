Looking after cars in Lyndhurst

If your paint in Lyndhurst still feels rough straight after a wash, that is bonded industrial fallout - tiny metal particles corroding into the clear coat. It needs dissolving out chemically, not scrubbing, which is core CDS work.

CDS is owner-operated. The person who looks at your paint is the person who corrects and coats it — Andy, with 30+ years of experience — so what you are quoted for Lyndhurst is exactly what gets done.

Beside an industrial belt

What Lyndhurst does to a car’s finish.

Rough paint on a clean car

The plastic-bag test tells you: run your hand over a washed panel inside a thin bag. If it feels like fine sandpaper, the paint is contaminated.

Rail dust

Rail lines shed fine metal dust that behaves exactly like industrial fallout and needs the same chemical removal.

Brake and road grit

Industrial roads carry a lot of grit and brake dust, which loads wheels and lower panels faster than a normal suburban street.

Parking at work all day

Cars left in an industrial car park all day take the full dose. A coating is what stops the contamination bonding while you are at work.

Can the work be done at my place in Lyndhurst?

Andy is mobile as well as studio-based, so the work can be done at your place in Lyndhurst or at the Berwick studio, about 11 km away. Work vehicles and fleets can be done at your workplace or depot so they are not off the road for a drop-off; private cars at home or in the studio, whichever is easier. Do you coat work utes and vans?

Yes, including trays, canopies and trim. A coated work vehicle takes a fraction of the time to clean.

Will fallout come back after it is removed?

The environment will keep putting it there, but on coated paint it struggles to bond and most of it comes off in a normal wash.

Is fallout the same as overspray?

No. Fallout is metal particle that corrodes into the clear coat; overspray is paint or similar material that bonds on top. They are removed differently, which is why we assess before touching the paint.
