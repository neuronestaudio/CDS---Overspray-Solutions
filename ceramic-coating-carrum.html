Carrum, and what it does to paint

Salt is the whole story in Carrum. Air off the water carries a fine saline film that settles on paint, trim and brightwork and keeps working on it long after the car looks dry.

There is no sales team and no subcontracting. Andy assesses, corrects and coats every car from Carrum himself, with more than thirty years on paint behind him, and will tell you honestly if a job is not worth doing.

Bayside and coastal

The things that wear paint down in Carrum.

Windscreen haze

Salt film on glass scatters light and makes night driving harder. Glass coating keeps it clearer and sheds water at speed.

Salt air

A fine saline film settles on the car constantly and attracts moisture. On a coated car it rinses off; on bare paint it sits and works away at it.

Sand in everything

Sand is abrasive. Wiping it off a panel scratches the paint, and it packs into sills, seat rails and carpet.

Reflected UV

Sun off the water adds to the direct UV load, which is what fades reds and blacks and chalks plastic trim.

The process

How a Carrum booking runs.

01

Assessment

We inspect the paint under proper lighting and measure the clear coat, then tell you what it actually needs.

02

Decontaminate

Iron-fallout treatment and a clay strip, so nothing is ever sealed in under the coating.

FAQs

Questions we get from Carrum.

Is CDS mobile in Carrum?

Andy works mobile in Carrum and from the Berwick studio, which is about 20 km from you, so it is your choice. Near the water it is salt air and the sea breeze that matter: a coating needs to cure dry and out of the wind. A closed garage does that; otherwise the studio does. Either way it is the same person, the same products and the same registered warranty.

How often should I wash a car near the water?

More often than inland - a thorough rinse after windy days and beach trips helps a lot. On a coated car that rinse does most of the work.

Can you coat the chrome and trim?

Yes. Brightwork and plastic trim suffer as much as paint by the water, and both can be coated.

I tow a boat. Anything I should know?

Rinse the lower panels, wheels and the tow bar area after every ramp run. Coated wheels and sills make that rinse far more effective.
