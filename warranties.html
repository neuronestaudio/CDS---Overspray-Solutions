What is covered

  • Loss of gloss and protective performance
  • Durability under normal use with correct care
  • UV, oxidation and chemical resistance
  • Hydrophobic (water-beading) performance

What is not covered

  • Stone chips, impact and accident damage
  • Abrasive automatic car washes and unapproved products
  • Incorrect wash technique and neglected maintenance
  • Misuse, and underlying clear-coat failure

Keeping the guarantee valid.

GYEON and ONYX warranties come with sensible conditions. Meet these and you're covered.

01

Certified application

Installed by a certified applicator using manufacturer-approved prep, products and cure.

02

Registration

Warranty registered with GYEON / ONYX, with any required inspections completed on time.

03

Correct maintenance

Two-bucket washing and the recommended maintenance schedule — no abrasive brush washes.

04

Annual service (lifetime)

The ONYX N1 lifetime cover is kept valid with an annual ONYX maintenance service.
