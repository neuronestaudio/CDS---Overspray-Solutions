What is covered

  • Coating durability and adhesion
  • Gloss retention
  • UV protection
  • Hydrophobic performance
  • Self-healing of light marring (Kraken tiers)

What is not covered

  • Stone chips and impact damage
  • Accident or collision damage
  • Abrasive or unapproved products
  • Hard-water spotting and neglected maintenance
  • Underlying clear-coat failure, tree sap and neglect
