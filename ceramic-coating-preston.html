Local to Preston

Preston mixes residential streets with working industry and the High Street strip, which is a harder environment for paint than it looks.

You get two problems at once here: tree sap on the residential streets and industrial fallout closer to the industrial end. They need different treatments — sap is polished out once it has etched, fallout has to be chemically dissolved. We assess which you actually have before touching the paint, then coat so neither keys in again.

Every Preston booking is handled by Andy himself — owner-operated, 30+ years on paint, and the same standard whether it is a daily driver, a work ute or a weekend car. Tell us the car and what is bothering you and you will get a straight answer, not a package sell.

The process

How a Preston booking runs.

01

Assessment

We look at your paint under proper lighting and measure the clear coat, then tell you what it actually needs.

02

Decontaminate

Iron-fallout treatment and a clay strip, so nothing is ever sealed in under the coating.

03

Correct

FAQs

Preston ceramic coating questions.

Do you come to Preston, or do I bring the car to you?

All work is done at our Epping studio, about 11 km from Preston. A coating has to cure in a clean, controlled, dust-free space to reach its rated life, which is not something a driveway can offer. We are open 7 days by appointment.

My car is parked on the street in Preston — is a coating still worth it?

That is exactly when it is most worth it. A kerbside car takes UV, sap and bird droppings with no shelter, and a coating seals the clear coat so that mess sits on the surface and lifts in a normal wash instead of etching in.

How long does a ceramic coating take?

Most coatings are a one to three day job depending on how much paint correction is needed. We will give you an exact timeframe for your car when we assess it, and you are usually safe to drive the day after the coating goes on.

What does ceramic coating cost in Preston?

It is quoted per car, because the price is driven by the size of the car, the condition of the paint and how many correction stages it needs — not by your postcode. Tell us the car and what is bothering you and we will give you a straight number.
