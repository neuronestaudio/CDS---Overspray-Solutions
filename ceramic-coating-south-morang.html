Local to South Morang

South Morang is commuter country — newer homes, the train line, and a lot of cars covering serious freeway kilometres every week.

Freeway driving lays down an oily traffic film that bonds to bare clear coat, which is why a car can look dirty again two days after a wash. It also means stone chipping at the front. We decontaminate that film off properly, correct what is fixable, then coat the paint so the grime rinses instead of sticking.

Every South Morang booking is handled by Andy himself — owner-operated, 30+ years on paint, and the same standard whether it is a daily driver, a work ute or a weekend car. Tell us the car and what is bothering you and you will get a straight answer, not a package sell.

Full sun, little shade

Newer South Morang streets have young trees and wide open frontages, so cars sit in unbroken sun most of the day.

Industrial fallout

Airborne metal particle from nearby industry settles on horizontal panels and corrodes into the clear coat as tiny rust specks.

New cars, no protection

A lot of South Morang cars are only a year or two old — the ideal time to seal paint, before the first wash marks go in.

Construction and estate dust

Building work around South Morang puts render, cutting dust and paint mist into the air — and it bonds to clear coat rather than washing off.

FAQs

South Morang ceramic coating questions.

Do you come to South Morang, or do I bring the car to you?

All work is done at our Epping studio, about 7 km from South Morang. A coating has to cure in a clean, controlled, dust-free space to reach its rated life, which is not something a driveway can offer. We are open 7 days by appointment.

There is building work around South Morang. Can you remove construction overspray?

Yes — overspray and fallout removal is our specialty. Render, paint mist and concrete slurry bond to clear coat and washing does nothing. We remove it non-abrasively so the clear coat is preserved, then coat the car so it stops keying in.

How long does a ceramic coating take?

Most coatings are a one to three day job depending on how much paint correction is needed. We will give you an exact timeframe for your car when we assess it, and you are usually safe to drive the day after the coating goes on.

What does ceramic coating cost in South Morang?

It is quoted per car, because the price is driven by the size of the car, the condition of the paint and how many correction stages it needs — not by your postcode. Tell us the car and what is bothering you and we will give you a straight number.
