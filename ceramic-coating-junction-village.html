New estates and growth corridors

What Junction Village does to a car’s finish.

Construction overspray

Paint mist from a house being finished next door drifts across the street and settles as a rough film. Washing does nothing; it has to be lifted off without cutting into the clear coat.

Render and concrete spatter

Tiny grey dots across a bonnet or windscreen are usually render or concrete slurry. Left alone they etch; caught early they come off cleanly.

Cutting and site dust

Brick and tile cutting throws up fine abrasive dust that settles on every car in the street. Wiping it off dry is how new paint gets scratched.

New cars, unprotected

A new car's paint is the best it will ever be on delivery day. Sealing it before the first wash mark goes in is the cheapest protection there is.

The process

From first look to final cure.

01

Assessment

We inspect the paint under proper lighting and measure the clear coat, then tell you what it actually needs.

02

Decontaminate

Iron-fallout treatment and a clay strip, so nothing is ever sealed in under the coating.

03

Correct

Machine polishing in measured stages to remove swirls and etching rather than fill them.

04

Coat & cure

GYEON or ONYX applied panel by panel and cured under cover — in the studio, or in your garage on a mobile job — with the warranty registered.

FAQs

Before you book from Junction Village.

Can the work be done at my place in Junction Village?

Andy is mobile as well as studio-based, so the work can be done at your place in Junction Village or at the Berwick studio, about 12 km away. In a new estate the issue is dust from the building sites around you. A mobile job is done with the garage door down for that reason; if the garage is still full of boxes, the studio is the cleaner option. Tell us where the car is kept when you book and you will get a straight answer on which suits the job.

There is building work in my street. Can you remove overspray?

Yes - it is our specialty. Overspray, render and concrete spatter are removed non-abrasively so the clear coat is preserved, then the paint is coated so the next round of site dust washes off.

Should I coat a brand-new car?

It is the best time to. The paint is undamaged, so there is little or nothing to correct and the coating bonds to a perfect surface.

Is the dealer's paint protection enough?

Usually not. Dealer packages are typically a quick spray sealant that lasts months. A certified GYEON or ONYX coating is a different class of product and lasts years.
