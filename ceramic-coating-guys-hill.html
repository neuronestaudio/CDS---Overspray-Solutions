Lifestyle acreage

The things that wear paint down in Guys Hill.

Horse floats and trailers

Towing floats and trailers throws mud and grit up the back of the car and onto the tow bar area.

Mud in the arches

Wet-season mud packs into wheel arches and sills and holds moisture against the metal.

Sun on open blocks

Open acreage means little shade and a lot of UV, which fades paint and chalks plastic trim.

Sheds and farm dust

Cars stored in sheds pick up hay dust, bird droppings from roosting birds and the odd rodent - interiors included.

FAQs

Guys Hill ceramic coating questions.

Can you coat the wheels?

Yes, and on dirt roads it is well worth it - brake dust and mud stop baking onto coated wheels.

Is a coating worth it on a dusty property?

Yes - it is one of the best places for one. Dust does not bond to sealed paint, so it rinses off instead of having to be wiped, which is what protects the finish.

Do you coat utes and 4WDs?

Yes, including trays, canopies, bars and raw plastics. They benefit as much as the paint does.
