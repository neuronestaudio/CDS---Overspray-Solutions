Local to Fairfield

Fairfield backs onto Yarra Bend, so it is leafier than most inner-north suburbs. Beautiful to live in, less kind to a clear coat.

Gum trees are the issue here. Eucalypt sap is sticky, acidic and sets hard, and once it has sat through a hot day it etches a ring into the clear coat that a wash will never remove. Coated paint gives you a window to get it off safely. If there are already sap rings in the paint, we polish them out before coating.

Every Fairfield booking is handled by Andy himself — owner-operated, 30+ years on paint, and the same standard whether it is a daily driver, a work ute or a weekend car. Tell us the car and what is bothering you and you will get a straight answer, not a package sell.

FAQs

Fairfield ceramic coating questions.

Do you come to Fairfield, or do I bring the car to you?

All work is done at our Epping studio, about 16 km from Fairfield. A coating has to cure in a clean, controlled, dust-free space to reach its rated life, which is not something a driveway can offer. We are open 7 days by appointment.

My car is parked on the street in Fairfield — is a coating still worth it?

That is exactly when it is most worth it. A kerbside car takes UV, sap and bird droppings with no shelter, and a coating seals the clear coat so that mess sits on the surface and lifts in a normal wash instead of etching in.

How long does a ceramic coating take?

Most coatings are a one to three day job depending on how much paint correction is needed. We will give you an exact timeframe for your car when we assess it, and you are usually safe to drive the day after the coating goes on.

What does ceramic coating cost in Fairfield?

It is quoted per car, because the price is driven by the size of the car, the condition of the paint and how many correction stages it needs — not by your postcode. Tell us the car and what is bothering you and we will give you a straight number.
