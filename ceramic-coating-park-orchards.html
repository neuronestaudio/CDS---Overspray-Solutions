Local to Park Orchards

Park Orchards is large leafy blocks and quiet unmade roads — one of the greenest pockets we cover, and one of the dustiest.

Unsealed roads plus heavy tree cover is the worst combination for paint, because abrasive dust settles onto sap that is already sticky. Wiping that off dry is how bonnets get destroyed. A coating means you can pressure-rinse it away instead, and it keeps the sap from bonding in the first place.

What your paint is up against in Park Orchards.

The things that actually wreck a finish around here — and what we do about them.

Eucalypt sap

Gums over Park Orchards driveways drop a sticky, acidic sap that sets hard and etches a ring into clear coat within a hot day.

Bark, leaf and litter

Heavy native tree cover means debris sitting on the paint constantly, holding moisture against it.

Dust from unsealed roads

Gravel driveways and unmade roads around Park Orchards put abrasive dust over the car — and wiping it dry is what scratches bonnets.

Damp, shaded parking

Shaded Park Orchards streets stay damp longer, which gives sap and fallout more time to bite into the surface.

FAQs

Park Orchards ceramic coating questions.

Do you come to Park Orchards, or do I bring the car to you?

All work is done at our Epping studio, about 24 km from Park Orchards. A coating has to cure in a clean, controlled, dust-free space to reach its rated life, which is not something a driveway can offer. We are open 7 days by appointment.

Gum trees hang over my driveway in Park Orchards. Will a coating stop sap damage?

It will not stop sap landing, but it stops it bonding. On sealed paint the sap sits on the surface, so you can remove it safely instead of finding an etched ring weeks later. Any rings already in the clear coat are polished out first.

How long does a ceramic coating take?

Most coatings are a one to three day job depending on how much paint correction is needed. We will give you an exact timeframe for your car when we assess it, and you are usually safe to drive the day after the coating goes on.

What does ceramic coating cost in Park Orchards?

It is quoted per car, because the price is driven by the size of the car, the condition of the paint and how many correction stages it needs — not by your postcode. Tell us the car and what is bothering you and we will give you a straight number.
