Baxter, and what it does to paint

Out around Baxter, a car is rarely clean for long. The goal is not a car that never gets dusty; it is a car that can be rinsed clean safely instead of wiped, because wiping dust off dry paint is how it gets scratched.

Every Baxter booking is handled by Andy himself — owner-operated, 30+ years on paint, and the same standard for a daily driver, a work ute or a weekend car. Tell us the car and what is bothering you and you will get a straight answer.

Lifestyle acreage

The things that wear paint down in Baxter.

Horse floats and trailers

Towing floats and trailers throws mud and grit up the back of the car and onto the tow bar area.

Mud in the arches

Wet-season mud packs into wheel arches and sills and holds moisture against the metal.

Sun on open blocks

Open acreage means little shade and a lot of UV, which fades paint and chalks plastic trim.

Sheds and farm dust

Cars stored in sheds pick up hay dust, bird droppings from roosting birds and the odd rodent - interiors included.

FAQs

Before you book from Baxter.

Will a coating stop stone chips?

No. Stone chips are impact damage and only paint protection film meaningfully reduces them. A coating seals the paint around them.

How far is the studio from Baxter?

About 25 km, in Berwick. The car comes in because a coating needs a clean, dust-free space to cure - a gravel drive is the opposite.

Can you clean out a car that lives in a shed?

Yes. Interior detailing handles dust, droppings and odour, and coating the fabric makes it easier to keep clean afterwards.
