Blue-chip and prestige

What Caulfield South does to a car’s finish.

Piano black and gloss trim

Gloss-black pillars, grilles and interior trim scratch if you look at them. They can be refined and coated along with the paint.

Matte and satin finishes

Matte and satin paint cannot be machine-polished - polishing makes it glossy and cannot be undone. Tell us before you book so the car is handled correctly from the first wash.

Paint protection film

Many cars here already carry film on the front end. Film and coating work well together, and a coating over the film makes it easier to keep clean.

Low-kilometre garage cars

Cars that do few kilometres still oxidise and gather dust marks from covers and wipe-downs. They are ideal candidates for a multi-year system because the paint under them is so good.

The process

How a Caulfield South booking runs.

01

Assessment

We inspect the paint under proper lighting and measure the clear coat, then tell you what it actually needs.

02

Decontaminate

Iron-fallout treatment and a clay strip, so nothing is ever sealed in under the coating.

03

Correct

Machine polishing in measured stages to remove swirls and etching rather than fill them.

04

Coat & cure

GYEON or ONYX applied panel by panel and cured under cover — in the studio, or in your garage on a mobile job — with the warranty registered.

FAQs

Questions we get from Caulfield South.

Can the work be done at my place in Caulfield South?

Andy is mobile as well as studio-based, so the work can be done at your place in Caulfield South or at the Berwick studio, about 33 km away. A garaged home suits a mobile visit well. For a multi-stage correction, where controlled lighting shows every last mark, the studio is the better room to work in. Tell us where the car is kept when you book and you will get a straight answer on which suits the job.

GYEON or ONYX for a prestige car?

For a garaged car where clarity in direct sun matters most we generally lean GYEON; for one that is driven daily and parked outside, ONYX 10H graphene for durability. Often either is a good answer and we will say so.

Can you coat a car that already has paint protection film?

Yes. The coating goes over the film as well as the paint. It adds chemical resistance the film lacks and makes the whole car easier to wash.

My car has matte paint. Can you help?

We can wash and protect it correctly, but matte paint cannot be polished, so scratches cannot be corrected the way they can on gloss. Mention it when you book so there are no surprises.
