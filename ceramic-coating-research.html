Local to Research

Research is semi-rural — big blocks, bush surrounds and a fair share of unsealed roads and driveways.

Dust is the defining issue. Fine road dust settles on the car constantly, and because it is abrasive, every casual wipe-down puts scratches in. This is the exact situation a coating is built for: the dust does not bond to a sealed surface, so it rinses off with water pressure alone rather than needing to be wiped.

Every Research booking is handled by Andy himself — owner-operated, 30+ years on paint, and the same standard whether it is a daily driver, a work ute or a weekend car. Tell us the car and what is bothering you and you will get a straight answer, not a package sell.

FAQs

Research ceramic coating questions.

Do you come to Research, or do I bring the car to you?

All work is done at our Epping studio, about 17 km from Research. A coating has to cure in a clean, controlled, dust-free space to reach its rated life, which is not something a driveway can offer. We are open 7 days by appointment.

Gum trees hang over my driveway in Research. Will a coating stop sap damage?

It will not stop sap landing, but it stops it bonding. On sealed paint the sap sits on the surface, so you can remove it safely instead of finding an etched ring weeks later. Any rings already in the clear coat are polished out first.

How long does a ceramic coating take?

Most coatings are a one to three day job depending on how much paint correction is needed. We will give you an exact timeframe for your car when we assess it, and you are usually safe to drive the day after the coating goes on.

What does ceramic coating cost in Research?

It is quoted per car, because the price is driven by the size of the car, the condition of the paint and how many correction stages it needs — not by your postcode. Tell us the car and what is bothering you and we will give you a straight number.
