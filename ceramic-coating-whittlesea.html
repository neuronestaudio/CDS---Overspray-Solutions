Local to Whittlesea

Whittlesea is a rural township at the top of the corridor — farmland, gravel, and cars that cover long distances on dusty roads.

Country-road driving puts an abrasive film across the whole car and a lot of grit into the lower panels. Sealing the paint is what makes that manageable: a coated car rinses clean, an uncoated one has to be wiped, and wiping grit is what creates the scratches. We coat wheels out here as standard too.

Every Whittlesea booking is handled by Andy himself — owner-operated, 30+ years on paint, and the same standard whether it is a daily driver, a work ute or a weekend car. Tell us the car and what is bothering you and you will get a straight answer, not a package sell.

FAQs

Whittlesea ceramic coating questions.

Do you come to Whittlesea, or do I bring the car to you?

All work is done at our Epping studio, about 22 km from Whittlesea. A coating has to cure in a clean, controlled, dust-free space to reach its rated life, which is not something a driveway can offer. We are open 7 days by appointment.

There is building work around Whittlesea. Can you remove construction overspray?

Yes — overspray and fallout removal is our specialty. Render, paint mist and concrete slurry bond to clear coat and washing does nothing. We remove it non-abrasively so the clear coat is preserved, then coat the car so it stops keying in.

How long does a ceramic coating take?

Most coatings are a one to three day job depending on how much paint correction is needed. We will give you an exact timeframe for your car when we assess it, and you are usually safe to drive the day after the coating goes on.

What does ceramic coating cost in Whittlesea?

It is quoted per car, because the price is driven by the size of the car, the condition of the paint and how many correction stages it needs — not by your postcode. Tell us the car and what is bothering you and we will give you a straight number.
