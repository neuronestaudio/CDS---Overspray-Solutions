FAQs

Langwarrin South ceramic coating questions.

Do you come to Langwarrin South, or do I bring the car in?

We can come to you in Langwarrin South, or you can bring the car to the studio in Berwick, roughly 22 km away - both are on offer. Acreage properties usually have the room, the shed and the power a mobile job needs, so on-site work suits most of what we do out here. If you are unsure, send a photo of where the car lives with your enquiry and we will work out the best option.

Will a coating stop stone chips?

No. Stone chips are impact damage and only paint protection film meaningfully reduces them. A coating seals the paint around them.

Can you clean out a car that lives in a shed?

Yes. Interior detailing handles dust, droppings and odour, and coating the fabric makes it easier to keep clean afterwards.

How should I wash a car that gets dusty every day?

Rinse first with plenty of water to lift the grit, then wash - never wipe dust off dry. On coated paint the rinse does most of the job.
